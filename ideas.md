# Tlholoes Funeral Parlour — design direction

## Selected approach — Quiet Heritage

The site uses a contemporary editorial memorial direction: clear information, restrained heritage framing, the supplied cross-and-laurel mark, and humane direct contact actions. The updated palette fuses **Tlholoes Red #B3082D**, **Ink Black #121212**, and **Clean White #FFFFFF**. Red is reserved for calls to action and recognition, black anchors serious information and the footer, and white keeps the reading surfaces open and legible.

## Style Decisions

The homepage uses the user-provided building-and-vehicles photograph as a hero image with a dark overlay and editorial copy. It is intentionally contained to the homepage hero rather than used as a site-wide background. Interior sections return to solid white, blush-white, or black surfaces so the photography supports the story without governing the entire site. The supplied logo remains framed as a high-contrast heritage seal.
